Privacy Policy for Dash Runner

Last Updated: October 27, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Dash Runner. We are committed to protecting the privacy of our players. This Privacy Policy explains our data collection and handling practices when you install and play Dash Runner on Android devices.

2. User Data Handling Summary

CategoryHandling Practice
Personal Information (Name, Email, Phone)Not Collected. No registration or login required.
Financial / Payment InfoNot Collected. 100% free; no in-app purchases.
Photos, Audio, Files, ContactsNot Accessed. No storage or device permissions requested.
Precise GPS LocationNot Accessed. Only coarse location inferred by AdMob from IP.
Game Progress & ScoresStored Locally. Saved exclusively on your device sandbox.
Advertising Identifiers (GAID)Collected by Google AdMob for ad delivery & fraud prevention.
Data SecurityEncrypted in transit via HTTPS / TLS.

3. Local Data Storage

All your game progression (unlocked levels 1–20, high scores, coins, power-up upgrade levels, audio settings) is stored strictly locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. This data is never uploaded to any server owned by the developer.

4. Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

Dash Runner integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to serve advertisements. AdMob may automatically collect pseudonymous identifiers (Google Advertising ID / GAID), coarse location inferred from IP, ad engagement metrics, and crash diagnostics to deliver ads and prevent fraud.

Review Google's Privacy Policy at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Permissions Requested

The app requests only normal install-time permissions: INTERNET (load ads), ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (check connectivity), and VIBRATE (haptic feedback). Dash Runner does not request camera, contacts, microphone, or storage access.

6. Data Deletion & Your Rights

You can erase all local game data at any time by clearing the app's storage under Android Settings ➔ Apps ➔ Dash Runner ➔ Storage ➔ Clear Data, or by using the in-game "Reset Saved Data" option. You can reset or delete your advertising identifier under Android Settings ➔ Google ➔ Ads.

7. Children's Privacy

Dash Runner is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
